## Brand & Style

This design system drives a two-sided subscription-sharing ecosystem where users act simultaneously as buyers and sellers. The visual direction balances the frictionlessness of peer-to-peer commerce with bank-grade reassurance. It breaks away from traditional fintech coldness by blending organic warmth, tactile soft neumorphism, and bold, energetic vibrancy.

### Visual Style
- **Warm Tactile Neumorphism:** Moving away from dated, gray-heavy neumorphism, this style uses an organic warm cream canvas paired with dual-source directional soft lighting (crisp white uplight highlights and deep ink diffused ambient shadows). Surfaces feel soft, sculpted, and inviting.
- **Vibrant Accent Energy:** High-voltage vivid rose pink serves as the primary catalyst for conversion, active states, and financial interactions. Deep berry undertones introduce dramatic editorial gradients across hero messaging and badges.
- **Tone of Voice & Mood:** Friendly, trustworthy, fluid, and delightfully tactile. The user experience feels personal, transparent, and effortlessly communal.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an organic cream ecosystem punctuated by radiant berry-rose accents and deep ink typography.

### Core Canvas & Neutral Palette
- **Base Canvas (`#F6F4EF`):** The primary cream foundation that creates warmth and softens visual fatigue.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FAF8F5` / `#FFFFFF`):** Slightly elevated cream surfaces and pure white top-tier planes that bounce soft directional light.
- **Primary Ink (`#14142B`):** Deep midnight ink used for display headlines, primary values, and high-emphasis interface elements.
- **Secondary Ink / Muted (`#62616D`):** Balanced mid-tone slate for supporting subtitles, input placeholders, and secondary metadata.
- **Subtle Borders & Dividers (`#EBE8DF`, `#E4E1D7`):** Hairline structure lines used selectively when elevation alone is insufficient.

### Primary Accents & Gradients
- **Vivid Rose Primary (`#FF3168`):** High-converting action color for buttons, key badges, active tabs, and focus rings.
- **Deep Berry Accent (`#910032`):** Low-end gradient anchor paired with `#FF3168` to produce rich linear fills for display titles and hero callouts.
- **Rose Glow (`rgba(255, 49, 104, 0.35)`): Atmospheric ambient halo applied beneath primary interactive buttons and key progress metrics.

### Dark Admin Theme Mode
- **Dark Canvas (`#14142B`):** Deep ink background for administrative, data-dense consoles.
- **Dark Elevated Surfaces (`#1B1B36`, `#181830`):** Soft midnight containers for analytics tables, revenue graphs, and user controls.
- **Dark Text High-Contrast (`#F6F4EF`):** Clean cream-white text ensuring complete WCAG AAA readability against dark midnight planes.
- **Dark Accents:** `#FF3168` maintained with subtle neon atmospheric spread.

## Typography

The type system blends the quirky, high-character geometry of **Bricolage Grotesque** with the ultra-clean legibility and humanism of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Expressive Display Treatments
- Key display titles and promotional statements in `Bricolage Grotesque` utilize an intentional background-clip linear gradient running at 135 degrees from `#FF3168` to `#910032`.
- Promotional section tags and financial milestone titles incorporate warm highlight badges alongside sparkle accents (✨) to signal dynamic shared revenue and savings.

### Body & Micro-Copy Rules
- All tabular data, form controls, transaction ledgers, and body text rely on `Plus Jakarta Sans` for optical balance and accessibility.
- Leading is deliberately relaxed (`1.5` to `1.6`) across body content to complement the pill-shaped elements and spacious neumorphic cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive grid with expansive breathing room, prioritizing comfortable click targets and legible dual-role dashboards (e.g., Buyer "My Subscriptions" vs. Seller "Slot Earnings").

### Breakpoints & Grid Adaptation
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1.5rem outer canvas margin, 1rem gutter. Content stacks linearly into organic card streams.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 2rem margin, 1.5rem gutter. Split layout displaying buyer feeds on one side and seller analytics on the other.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns with a max content container width of 1280px, 3rem outer margins, and 2rem gutters.

### Spacing Philosophy
Neumorphic dual shadows need ample physical clearance to prevent visual overlap and mud. Outer card margins are never smaller than `space-md` (`1rem`), and card internal padding scales between `1.5rem` on mobile and `2rem` on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

This system achieves hierarchy through warm directional light modeling rather than harsh drop shadows or flat borders.

### Soft Neumorphic Formula (Light Mode)
- **Standard Neumorphic Card / Panel:**
  `box-shadow: -4px -4px 12px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85), 6px 6px 16px rgba(20, 20, 43, 0.06);`
  This dual-source signature creates a gentle tactile lift from the `#F6F4EF` cream base without creating dirty grays.
- **Pressed / Inset State (Active Cards, Input Wells):**
  `box-shadow: inset 3px 3px 8px rgba(20, 20, 43, 0.06), inset -3px -3px 8px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7);`

### Vivid Atmospheric Glow
- **Primary Interactive Glow:**
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(255, 49, 104, 0.35);`
  Used specifically for main conversion buttons, active subscription badges, and live transaction highlights.

### Dark Admin Theme Elevation
- Ambient shadows transition to deep midnight casts:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35), 0 2px 4px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04);`
- Surfaces use `#1B1B36` and `#181830` with `1px` subtle rim outlines (`rgba(246, 244, 239, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

The design system is unapologetically organic and friendly, adopting high-radius curvature across all functional elements.

### Curvature Hierarchy
- **Pill Primitives (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** All interactive buttons, chip selectors, input fields, and status badges use pill profiles.
- **Organic Large Cards (`rounded-[28px]`):** Subscription cards, user profile panels, and transaction modules use a generous 28px border radius to reinforce soft tactile surfaces.
- **Micro-Containers (`rounded-2xl` / `16px`):** Avatar containers, thumbnail covers of subscription services (e.g., Netflix, Spotify icons), and tooltip wrappers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** `rounded-full`, background `#FF3168`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` 600. Features the signature vivid glow: `shadow-[0_8px_24px_rgba(255,49,104,0.35)]`. On hover: `scale-[1.02]` with an active outer ring `ring-4 ring-[#FF3168]/20`.
- **Secondary / Neumorphic Button:** `rounded-full`, background `#FAF8F5`, text `#14142B`, dual soft shadow (`-4px -4px 12px rgba(255,255,255,0.85), 6px 6px 16px rgba(20,20,43,0.06)`). On hover: `scale-[1.01]` with inset text color shifting to `#FF3168`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** `rounded-full`, background `#FAF8F5`, subtle internal recess shadow, border `1px solid #EBE8DF`, text `#14142B`, placeholder `#62616D`. Focus state transitions border to `#FF3168` with `ring-4 ring-[#FF3168]/15`.
- **Toggle Switches (Buyer / Seller Mode Switcher):** Capsule pill container with cream base. The sliding thumb features a soft elevated white circle with micro-shadowing and vivid rose text for the selected role.

### Cards & Marketplace Items
- **Subscription Card:** `rounded-[28px]`, background `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF8F5`, soft dual neumorphic shadow, padding `1.5rem` to `2rem`. Contains the service icon in `rounded-2xl`, slot availability pill badge, and live buyer-seller counter.
- **Hover Microinteraction:** Hovering a card triggers a gentle `translate-y-[-4px]` lift and enhances the dual ambient shadow diffusion.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** `rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
  - *Active / Available:* Background `rgba(255, 49, 104, 0.1)`, text `#FF3168`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 49, 104, 0.2)`.
  - *Neutral / Filled:* Background `#EBE8DF`, text `#62616D`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** Circular pill-matched geometry (`rounded-full`), `22px` diameter, background `#FAF8F5`, dual subtle shadow. Checked state fills with `#FF3168`, housing a centered white micro checkmark and soft outer glow.

### Iconography
- System uses clean outline glyphs from **Material Symbols Outlined** at a uniform 20px or 24px bounding box, stroke weight 400, rendered in `#14142B` or `#FF3168` for active statuses.
